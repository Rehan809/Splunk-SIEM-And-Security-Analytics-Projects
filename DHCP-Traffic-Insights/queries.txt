PROJECT 1: DHCP TRAFFIC INSIGHTS

1. DHCP Lease Time Analysis
index=dhcp sourcetype=dhcp_logs
| table _time client_ip lease_time

2. Client IP and MAC Address Mapping
index=dhcp sourcetype=dhcp_logs
| table _time client_ip client_mac

3. Inspect Raw DHCP Events
index=dhcp sourcetype=dhcp_logs
| head 10

4. Total DHCP Requests
index=dhcp sourcetype=dhcp_logs
| stats count as total_dhcp_requests

5. DHCP Requests Over Time
index=dhcp sourcetype=dhcp_logs
| timechart span=1h count

6. Top Clients by Request Count
index=dhcp sourcetype=dhcp_logs
| stats count as request_count by client_mac
| sort - request_count

7. Most Frequently Assigned IP Addresses
index=dhcp sourcetype=dhcp_logs
| stats count as assignment_count by client_ip
| sort - assignment_count

8. Unique MAC Addresses Over Time
index=dhcp sourcetype=dhcp_logs
| timechart span=1h dc(client_mac) as unique_mac_addresses
